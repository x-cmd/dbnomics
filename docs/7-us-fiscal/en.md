# US fiscal lines

Three lines: government revenue (mostly taxes), expenditure, and net lending (the deficit), all as % of GDP. The revenue–spending gap is the deficit — the source of debt accumulation.

Reading guide:

- Revenue flat (~17–19%) while spending trends up — a structural deficit;
- Crisis spikes in 2009 and 2020 (stimulus) pushed deficits to ~10–15%;
- Net lending negative almost always: balanced budgets are rare even in peacetime.
