# Policy rates

Borrow money from a bank for one night, and the price you pay is the policy rate. It is the one number that every mortgage, savings account and house price in the country leans on. It also sets the exchange rate, and it feeds into what stocks are worth. Change it, and the effect reaches your wage packet months later.

**Judgment**: the last decade reads in three acts. First, a slow return to normal after the financial crisis (2015–19). Second, the epic 2022–23 hikes against inflation, with the Fed going from 0 to 5.5%. Third, the last exit: the Bank of Japan leaving ultra-easy policy in 2024, ending a 30-year experiment. The ECB followed the Fed, but in smaller steps. Watch the shape of the BoJ line. It sits just below zero for years, then turns positive in 2024. The exit is visible in the moment the line leaves the basement.
