# Unemployment

Unemployment rate = jobless people actively seeking work / labour force. Okun's law: each point above the natural rate costs ~2 points of real GDP — a thermometer for the output gap.

Reading guide:

- Southern Europe (Spain ~11%) vs Japan (~2.5%): huge structural gaps — labour institutions, firing costs, youth employment;
- 2020 spike and rapid回落 — unusually tight labour markets in this recovery;
- Euro area >7%: one monetary policy, N labour markets.
