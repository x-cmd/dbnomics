# Inflation

Inflation is a sustained rise in the general price level, measured by CPI year-on-year. "Moderate (~2%)" is most central banks' target; too high erodes purchasing power, negative (deflation) suppresses consumption and investment.

Reading guide:

- The 2021–2023 global surge: supply chains + energy + easy policy; US/Euro peaks at 8–9%;
- Japan persistently below target (the mirror of a deflation trap); China near zero in 2023–24 — a weak-demand signal;
- Disinflation ≠ falling prices, just slower increases.
