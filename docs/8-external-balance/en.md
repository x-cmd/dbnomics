# Current account

The current account (CA) = goods & services trade + primary income + transfers, as % of GDP. It is what a country sells to the world minus what it buys, and the mirror of cross-border lending (a CA surplus = net lending abroad).

Reading guide:

- China's surplus narrowed but holds at 1–2%; Germany runs 6–8% for years (a friction point inside the euro area);
- The US deficit ~3% and persistent — the "exorbitant privilege": reserve-currency status lets it finance deficits indefinitely;
- The world should sum to zero; it never does (the statistical black hole).
