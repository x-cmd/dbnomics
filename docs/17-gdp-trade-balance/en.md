# Growth and the external balance

"Surplus = competitive, deficit = decline" is intuitive and wrong. x = real growth, y = current account; each line is a country's annual path 2000–25.

**Judgment**: three cases dismantle the intuition — the US (high growth + deficit): the strongest demand, importing the world's savings via dollar status; the Euro Area (low growth + 2-3% surplus): weak domestic demand, growth by exports (Germany its engine); China (high growth → narrowing surplus): rebalancing toward domestic demand. The external balance mirrors the savings–investment gap (see demography & savings), not a competitiveness scoreboard.
